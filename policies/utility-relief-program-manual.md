# Utility Relief Program Manual

Version 1.0 — fictional demonstration policy.

## Section 4.2 Required documents

A caseworker must verify a current utility statement, household income evidence,
and any available disconnection notice. A missing notice must be recorded as a
document gap, not treated as an automatic denial.

## Section 6.1 Decision authority

Only an authorized program decision-maker may determine eligibility or approve a
benefit amount. CaseAssist may summarize evidence but must not make or
communicate the determination.
